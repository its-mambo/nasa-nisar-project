WHERE YOU’RE EXPLORING

Wetlands in the Aral Sea region

The saved study footprint includes the published location of the Sudochye lake system, a wetland of international importance in Karakalpakstan. The selection covers a wider landscape; it is not the wetland’s official boundary.

For an environmental analyst, this is a starting point for choosing locations to verify. For a curious explorer, it is a way to compare the same landscape across a season.

    About Frequency A

    GCOV files organize radar measurements into groups named frequencyA and, for some acquisitions, frequencyB.

    This analysis compares the same frequency group at both dates, using HH and HV radar measurements. The group is determined by the loaded model.
